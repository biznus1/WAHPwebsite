## Visual direction

The site is institutional rather than startup. Navy anchors credibility, the light Paper colour gives editorial calm, and green appears only on calls to action. The emblem's ring recurs as a motif: the shared-operations core in the floor schematic, the hub of the platform diagram, the Accra rings and the watermark on navy panels. This ties the graphics to the logo without decorating with it.
